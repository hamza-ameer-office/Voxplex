Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Applies to Voxplex for macOS

Voxplex turns text into natural speech, speech into text, and helps you transcribe, summarize and translate your documents, audio and video. This policy explains what information Voxplex handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version.
  • No account, no sign-in, no sign-up.
  • Your library, history and settings are stored on your Mac, inside the app’s sandbox.
  • Some features (for example AI summarization, translation, cloud transcription and premium voices) need to send the content you choose to a third-party service so that it can be processed. Voxplex tells you this and asks for your permission before it sends anything.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

3. Permissions on your Mac

Voxplex runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. macOS asks you before the app can use any of the following, and you can change your choice at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

  • Microphone. Used only while you are recording in Speech to Text. Recording starts when you choose to start and stops when you stop. Voxplex does not listen in the background.
  • Speech Recognition. Used to turn your voice into text. Depending on the feature and your Mac, recognition may happen on the device or be handled by a service described in section 6.
  • Files and folders. Voxplex opens only the files you choose with the file picker or drag in from Finder. To remember your choice between launches, macOS keeps a permission token (a security-scoped bookmark) on your Mac. Voxplex does not scan your disk and does not edit, move or delete your original files.
  • Network. Used for purchases, for features that rely on a third-party service, and when you open a link.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the features you ask for: reading text aloud, converting speech to text, transcribing audio and video, summarizing and translating.
  • To show your Library, recent items and playback progress.
  • To unlock Premium features you have purchased.
  • To answer your support requests, fix bugs and improve the app.
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect the app and other people from abuse.

We do not use the content you process to build advertising profiles. We do not use your content to train our own or any third party’s AI models, and we require our service providers not to do so either.

5. Legal bases (EU/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performing our contract with you (providing the features you ask for), your consent (microphone access and sending content to third-party services, which you can withdraw at any time), our legitimate interests (keeping the app secure and working), and legal obligation.

6. Third-party services and how your data flows

Some Voxplex features cannot run fully on your Mac. When you use them, Voxplex sends only the content needed for that one request to a service provider, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS), and receives the result back. Voxplex shows a notice and asks for your permission the first time a feature needs to send content off your Mac, and you can decline. If you decline, that feature will not run.

How we manage these providers:

  • They act on our instructions, under written agreements that require confidentiality, security and use of your content only to complete your request.
  • We do not allow them to use your content to train their models or to build profiles of you, and we choose provider settings that turn such use off wherever the provider offers that setting.
  • Your content is not kept on our servers beyond what is needed to complete your request. Providers may keep short-lived technical logs, for example to prevent abuse, under their own policies.
  • We do not sell your content or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • A current list of the providers we use is available on request at mhamzaameer9@gmail.com. Each provider’s own privacy policy applies to the processing it does.

Please think before you send. Do not send documents, recordings or text that contain information you are not comfortable sharing with a third-party service, such as passwords, financial or medical records, or confidential material that you have no right to share. If you choose to process other people’s speech or documents, you are responsible for having their permission (see our Terms of Use).

7. Storage, retention and deletion

  • On your Mac. Your files, transcripts, summaries, translations and generated audio stay on your Mac until you delete them. Deleting an item moves it to the app’s Trash; emptying the Trash removes it permanently from the app’s storage. Deleting Voxplex removes the app’s sandbox data.
  • With service providers. Content sent for processing is kept only as long as needed to complete the request, and then deleted, except for short-lived security or abuse logs.
  • Support emails. Kept only as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal obligations.
  • Backups. If you back up your Mac (for example with Time Machine or iCloud), the app’s data may be included. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

Voxplex offers optional Premium features through Apple’s In-App Purchase. All payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations, refunds and restoring of purchases are handled by Apple under the Apple Media Services terms. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, card number or billing address. The app only checks with Apple whether Premium is active. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that processing: apple.com/legal/privacy. We may see aggregated sales and subscription reports from Apple that do not identify you.

9. Analytics, advertising and tracking

Voxplex contains no advertising, no ad networks, and no third-party tracking or attribution SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and Voxplex does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track. If you choose to share analytics or crash reports with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated reports.

10. Selling and sharing data

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined in California law. We disclose information only to the service providers described above, when you ask us to, or when the law requires.

11. Children

Voxplex is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process content in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent protections to keep your information protected.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the UAE Federal Decree-Law on Personal Data Protection, or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights.

Because Voxplex stores your data on your Mac and has no accounts, most of your data is under your direct control: you can view and delete it in the app and in macOS. To make a request about data we hold (for example, your support emails), write to mhamzaameer9@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it is you, and we will reply within the time the law requires.

14. Security

Your data on your Mac is protected by macOS and the App Sandbox, and data in transit to service providers is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Please keep your Mac updated and protect it with a password and FileVault disk encryption.

15. AI-generated results

Summaries, translations, transcripts and synthetic voices are produced by automated systems and can contain errors or omissions. Please check important results before relying on them. See our Terms of Use for details.
