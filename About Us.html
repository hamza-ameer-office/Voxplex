About Us

Voxplex is a voice and language toolkit for your Mac: listen to your documents, dictate by voice, transcribe recordings, summarize long text and translate it, all in one simple app.

Listen to anything

Turn documents, pasted text and images with text into natural-sounding speech.

Speak, and it types

Dictate with your microphone and watch your words appear as you talk.

Understand faster

Summaries and translations help you get to the point and cross language barriers.

What Voxplex does

  • Text to Speech. Import a document or image, or paste text, and have it read aloud. Choose a voice, language and playback speed, and pick up where you left off with Continue Listening.
  • Speech to Text. Record with your microphone and see a live transcript. Pause, resume, then copy, share or export the text.
  • Transcription. Drag in an audio or video file and get a transcript with timestamps that you can read alongside your media.
  • AI Summarization. Turn long documents and transcripts into a short summary, a detailed summary or a list of key points.
  • Translation. Translate text between languages with the original and the translation side by side.
  • Library. Keep your results organized by type, search them, and manage them with a Trash for items you delete.
  • Look and feel. A clean Mac-native interface that follows your Mac’s light and dark appearance.

What we believe

  • You decide what leaves your Mac. No account is needed. Your library lives on your Mac, and Voxplex asks before it sends any content to a service that helps process it.
  • No ads, no tracking. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Honest about AI. Automated transcripts, summaries and translations can be wrong. We tell you so, and we recommend checking anything important.
  • Fair pricing. Core features are free with limits. Premium unlocks more, as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription handled by Apple, with no account needed.
